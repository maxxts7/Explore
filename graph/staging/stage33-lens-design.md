## New themes (9)

1. **behavior-as-a-direction** — "A behavior becomes a direction in activation space"
   Claim: CAA's central move is representational: a high-level behavior is isolated as a
   single residual-stream direction by differencing paired prompts that differ by one
   answer token, then dosed with a signed multiplier. The theme walks the machinery that
   makes the direction clean (mean difference over hundreds of pairs, the A/B format,
   norm standardization) and the proposed refinements of where/when the vector is applied.
   Members: steering-vector, contrastive-activation-addition, caa-steering-vector-construction,
   mean-difference-method, multiple-choice-contrast-pair-format, steering-multiplier,
   steering-vector-normalization, residual-stream, targeted-token-steering,
   steering-outside-residual-stream

2. **activation-engineering-family** — "The activation engineering family: from single pairs to datasets"
   Claim: CAA is one member of a 2022–23 family of activation-space interventions; the
   theme reads the family as a sequence of refinements — single prompt pair (ActAdd) to
   dataset averages (CAA), attention heads (ITI) to residual stream, representation
   reading (RepE) to steering — and states what each design choice buys.
   Members: activation-engineering, activation-addition, inference-time-intervention,
   representation-engineering-zou, in-context-vectors, steering-vector,
   contrastive-activation-addition

3. **alignment-behaviors-made-dials** — "Seven alignment worries become seven dials"
   Claim: the behaviors alignment literature worries about in prose — sycophancy, refusal,
   corrigibility, self-preservation, myopia, coordination with other AIs, hallucination —
   become measurable, steerable quantities with a sign and a magnitude. The catalog is the
   corpus's failure modes operationalized.
   Members: sycophancy, refusal-behavior, corrigibility, ai-coordination-behavior,
   myopic-reward-behavior, survival-instinct-behavior, hallucination

4. **behavioral-contrast-datasets** — "Writing behavior down as A/B questions"
   Claim: steering quality is dataset quality — before a behavior can be steered it must
   be captured as a contrastive question set, sourced from Anthropic's human-written
   evaluations or generated by GPT-4, and validated (answer conditioning) to actually
   elicit the behavior.
   Members: advanced-ai-risk-dataset, sycophancy-eval-datasets-anthropic,
   caa-hallucination-dataset, caa-refusal-dataset, multiple-choice-contrast-pair-format, gpt-4

5. **steering-vs-training-levers** — "Three levers on one model: prompting, finetuning, steering"
   Claim: CAA composes with rather than replaces the training-time stack — it adds control
   on top of system prompts and on top of finetuning, at a fraction of the compute, and
   generalizes out-of-distribution where finetuning fails (sycophancy). Inference-time
   steering is a third lever, not a replacement for the first two.
   Members: caa-system-prompt-comparison, caa-finetuning-comparison,
   caa-computational-efficiency, system-prompting, few-shot-prompting,
   supervised-fine-tuning, contrastive-activation-addition

6. **judging-steered-outputs** — "Evaluating steering: behavior scores and capability floors"
   Claim: proving a steering method works needs two kinds of evidence — that the behavior
   moved (held-out multiple-choice probabilities, GPT-4-rated open generation) and that
   nothing else broke (MMLU as capability floor, TruthfulQA as truthfulness cross-check).
   Members: multiple-choice-behavioral-evaluation, open-ended-generation-evaluation,
   gpt-4, mmlu, truthfulqa

7. **reading-the-residual-stream** — "Steering doubles as interpretability"
   Claim: every steering artifact is also a measurement of the model's internals — PCA
   clustering shows where behavioral representations emerge (suddenly, about a third of
   the way through), token-level similarity shows vectors act as feature detectors,
   inter-layer transfer shows the direction is a general representation, all evidence for
   the linear representation hypothesis.
   Members: activation-pca-visualization, behavioral-clustering, letter-clustering, pca,
   token-level-cosine-similarity-analysis, inter-layer-similarity-and-transfer,
   linear-representation-hypothesis, linear-representations-of-sentiment, residual-stream

8. **what-rlhf-leaves-behind** — "Base versus chat: what RLHF changes inside"
   Claim: steering vectors double as an instrument for measuring RLHF itself — vectors
   extracted from the base model transfer into the chat model, most strongly at layers
   7–15, evidence that RLHF re-weights behavior while preserving mid-layer
   representations rather than rewriting them.
   Members: base-chat-representation-comparison, llama-2, llama-2-chat, rlhf

9. **steering-as-audit** — "Elicitation as audit: steering for red-teaming"
   Claim: if a small internal perturbation can elicit a behavior, adversarial inputs
   probably can too — steering inverts from control tool to audit tool, a cheaper
   systematic alternative to hunting jailbreaks, and the inability to elicit becomes a
   stronger safety guarantee than output sampling.
   Members: caa-red-teaming-application, jailbreaks, red-teaming

## Superthemes

- NEW **steering-and-model-internals** — "Steering the model from inside: activation
  engineering and what it reveals"
  Claim: the 2023 paper adds a lever unlike everything else in the corpus — it acts on
  activations at inference, not on weights or data at training time — and the same lever
  read backwards is an instrument for seeing how behaviors are represented and what RLHF
  actually changed.
  Members: all 9 new themes.
- scalable-oversight-to-rlhf-pipeline += steering-vs-training-levers, what-rlhf-leaves-behind
  (the pipeline supertheme gains the two lenses that look at the finished RLHF stack from
  outside: what a post-training lever adds on top of it, and what it left behind in the
  network's internals). Narrative revised accordingly.
