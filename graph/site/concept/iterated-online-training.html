Iterated (Online) Training

Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback — inherited

A training regime from prior work in which the preference model is periodically refreshed with new comparison data collected on the current policy's outputs, keeping the PM on-distribution; noted as a future direction now automatable with AI feedback.

Preferences drift. A preference model trained once and left alone can fall out of step with a policy that keeps changing during training, and iterated training is the older fix, periodically refreshing the preference model on the current policy's own outputs. It predates Constitutional AI.

What the technique is gets explained first, then why it is mentioned rather than actually used here, as a future direction that AI feedback could make easier to automate, before connecting to RL from AI Feedback.

What it is

Iterated (online) training is a refresh loop for a preference model that predates Constitutional AI, attributed by the paper to Bai et al. (2022): new comparison data is periodically collected on the current policy's own outputs and used to retrain or update the preference model, keeping it evaluating the same distribution of responses the policy is actually producing rather than an older, increasingly stale distribution.

Why it's flagged rather than used

Constitutional AI does not implement this in its own experiments; it names it as a future direction made newly practical by RL from AI Feedback: "we should be able to perform iterated 'online' training...with AI supervision, where we update the preference model with new AI feedback in order to keep it on the same distribution as the policy produces...by using AI feedback we can fully automate the process" (constitutional-ai, §"6.1 Future Directions", p. 15). RLAIF's contribution here is not the refresh idea itself but removing its bottleneck: once an AI feedback model rather than a human crowdworker supplies the comparisons, the refresh loop no longer needs continuous human labor and can in principle run indefinitely (RL from AI Feedback (RLAIF) — makes newly automatable → Iterated (Online) Training). This sits at the far end of the From an open problem to reward models to AI feedback theme's throughline from a named open problem to a scaled solution, and is one more instance of the Replacing the human in the loop at exactly the point it was the bottleneck connective theme's recurring move: an AI process substituting for a human one at exactly the point human throughput was the constraint.
