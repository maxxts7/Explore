Formal-methods-imported's link to distributional shift splits along a fault line neither theme names on its own. Model repair is different: the paper introduces it only inside the contracts discussion, as the technique for restoring a violated contract after the fact by altering an already-trained model, rather than switching to a pre-built conservative policy (concrete-problems, §"A unifying view: counterfactual reasoning and machine learning with contracts.", p. 19). Formal-methods-imported therefore reaches shift-as-contracts-and-causation through exactly one of its six members, and that member does a structurally different job, post-hoc repair of the model itself, than the two control-theoretic tools the same toolkit lends to statistical-relaxations-for-distributional-shift's detection problem instead.
